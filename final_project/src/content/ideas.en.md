# Guixu 归墟

![Five floating mountains around the cloud whirlpool of Guixu](five-islands.webp)

Five mountains float on a sea of cloud. Below them lies Guixu, a bottomless abyss that all water and light eventually drain into, and that never fills. The mountains drift with the currents, and as they drift, their climate, plants and creatures change.

Nothing here is modelled by hand. Every island, cliff, waterfall and pool is generated from rules and a handful of numbers, so the same rules can grow every mountain.

## Where the ideas come from

**Structure: the _Liezi_, "The Questions of Tang".** East of the Bohai Sea is a bottomless valley called Guixu. Five mountains float above it with their roots touching nothing, so they drift with the tides. [Liezi on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liezi)

**Contents: the _Shanhaijing_, Classic of Mountains and Seas.** Its entries follow a fixed pattern: a mountain, what grows and lives on it, what happens when you eat or wear it, where its river flows. That pattern is already a recipe, so the world can write new entries of its own. [Classic of Mountains and Seas on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classic_of_Mountains_and_Seas)

**Look: mineral pigment, game light.** The hues come from blue-green landscape painting and the azurite, malachite and ochre of the Dunhuang murals; the light comes from games like Sky and Journey, with a low sun, backlit edges and warm against cool. The landforms come from Huangshan, Zhangjiajie, Guilin, the Yuanyang terraces and the Jiuzhaigou pools. [A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Thousand_Li_of_Rivers_and_Mountains)

## Five mountains, one grammar

Every island has a broad plateau, sheer cliffs, rock hanging into the cloud and thin falls, but each has its own landform and light. Penglai, with stone peaks on a meadow, is the first one built. Others could be rice terraces that mirror the sky, or turquoise pools spilling over their rims.
