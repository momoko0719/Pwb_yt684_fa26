# White Jade creature: posture review and needs proposal (2026-11-14)

## Anatomy reference and current fault

The horse is a mechanical reference for the neck and shoulder, not a species model for the mythical creature. A [peer-reviewed equine neck motion study](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-2615/15/15/2259) tracks the head, each cervical vertebra and the first thoracic vertebra separately; head/neck motion is not one uniform hinge. [Horse locomotion research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2768006/) describes the neck's role in head stabilization and the large motion at the skull–neck junction. [Giraffe cervicothoracic research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4785981/) shows why movement at the neck–thorax junction matters when a long-necked quadruped lowers its head. These support a visual rig with a stable chest, a lower neck bend and a separate head pivot; they do not give this fictional beast exact joint limits.

In our code, `BODY.head.at` still describes the old 0.85 m blockout, while the generated mesh's neck stations span about 0.4–0.5 m. The live animation aims that shorter sculpt at the old simulation muzzle. The standing correction was disabled during rest, so the skull/throat were pulled into a large hanging chin. Drinking still asks a two-bone visual rig to reach a distant old-model water point; uniform stretch also enlarges the neck's thickness. The four-legged walking pose is already accepted by the owner and should stay as baseline.

## A concrete needs loop to review before implementation

[Equilinox](https://www.equilinox.com/about/) makes animal needs and plant placement visible to the player. The official [NetLogo Wolf Sheep Predation model](https://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/models/WolfSheepPredation) is a useful minimal procedural precedent: eating changes an animal's energy, eaten grass enters a regrowth state, and the regrowth time changes population behavior. We should adapt the **causal loop**, not copy sheep/wolf reproduction or a health-bar game.

1. Keep one deterministic world clock (`d.time`) and two bounded internal values, `thirst` and `hunger` (0–1). They decline with simulated time and movement. Use thresholds with hysteresis (e.g. go drink below 0.30, stop above 0.85) so it does not rapidly switch between goals. The solar lighting clock stays separate.
2. Replace the fixed rest/drink departure timer with a small priority selector: imminent water need → an existing valid shore stand; food need → a reachable grass-rich dry patch; otherwise rest/roam. Preserve the current route solver and all foot/water safety checks. If no valid food patch exists, continue safe rest rather than teleporting or starving immediately.
3. At a food patch, lower the head only when its mouth reaches actual grass. Decrease that patch's biomass while the animal eats; increase hunger value. Add `eat` as a distinct activity and contact event. A visible grazed patch and a recovering patch should make the cause apparent without a numerical HUD always on screen.
4. The current grass is deterministically generated by `CoverGrass.tsx` and `CoverField`; individual blades have no persistent lifecycle. Add a **coarse grazing-biomass field** over grass-suitable ground, separate from the immutable seeded suitability. Each cell has capacity `K` from moisture/soil suitability and current biomass `B`. Eating subtracts biomass. Between bites, recover toward capacity with `B += (K-B)*(1-exp(-r*dt))`, with slower `r` in dry cells. A positive seed bank lets bare-looking patches return. This is an artistic regrowth rule, not a calibrated ecological model.
5. Sample the same biomass field in near-grass and far-cover rendering. Prefer a small dynamic texture/uniform so grazing does not repeatedly rebuild 24 m grass chunks. The creature's selected card can show three linked cues: thirsty → walks to water; hungry → chooses patch; grazed patch → grows back. A short time-lapse toggle can make recovery observable in a classroom demo.

**First implementation slice:** one seeded grazing patch, a mouth-to-grass check, a biomass change visible in near and far cover, and deterministic recovery. Then connect hunger to goal selection. Test seeds 684, 42, 7 and 5676, including no-grass/no-water fallbacks, 1×/10×/30× consistency, route/foot safety, and whether biomass changes without camera-dependent chunk rebuilds. Keep the existing fixed schedule available in development until the needs loop passes those checks.
