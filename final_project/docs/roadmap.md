## Deferred: weather as the 24 solar terms (二十四节气)

Agreed on 2026-09-30 as the way weather should work, to be built later (not for the Oct 1 class).

- A year dial of 24 solar terms next to the time of day; draggable, with an optional "time flows"
  mode so days and terms advance on their own (the course asks for a persistent, ever-changing
  world).
- Each term drives a few named quantities, each with a simple rule:
  - sunlight: sun height and day length (high at the summer solstice, low at the winter solstice);
  - warmth plus rain, **accumulated** over time: grass grows in spring, is lush in summer, turns
    gold in autumn, thins in winter (growth with memory; ecosystems, session 7);
  - rain (雨水, 谷雨, the plum rains): pool levels, the number and width of falls; shallow or
    frozen water in winter;
  - flowering: Zhuyu blooms only in its season (for example 惊蛰 to 白露), on top of its daily rhythm;
  - cold (霜降 to 立春): a snow line coming down the peaks (the glazed-tile look);
  - monsoon: wind direction and strength by season (northwest in winter, southeast in summer).
- Later, each drifting island can run its terms with an offset, which is the world concept's
  "position determines climate".
- The meadow line and grass density sliders could then be derived from the term instead.
